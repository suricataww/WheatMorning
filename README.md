# WheatMorning
Hello there! Wheat Morning is a little cozy cooking game still in development phase, built from scratch by me. I would love you to play it test it out!

How do you play the game?
After pressing the play button, you will be shown a main menu:
- "Kitchen": click here to go to the kitchen and feed the character your delicious treats after baking them by opening the cabinet door and dragging the pastries to the character using your mouse cursor
- "Garden": here is where the culinary magic happens. You can browse the cookbook by pressing on the down left and right corners to switch pages, and once you've decided what you want to cook, just press the image inside the book! Then, drag and drop each ingredient into the bowl, in the order indicated on the list in the center of the screen. Once ready, just click on the bowl and the treat will cook itself!
- "Shop": In order to cook, you have to buy the ingredients first. You will be shown a menu, then just click on the button of the ingredient that you want to buy. Customers will pay you so that you can keep cooking.

I made this game using Godot game engine for coding and Autodesk Sketchbook for the illustrations. This game is in very early development, so I focused more on creating the basic mechanisms like drag and drop and a little animation, but there's more to come in future updates!
Sounds used in this game are free, from pixabay.com.
